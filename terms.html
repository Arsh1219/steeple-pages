What Bible Verse is

Bible Verse gives you a daily feed of Bible verses, prayers, quotes and affirmations, widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, reminders, a reading streak, a Prayer chat, voices that read aloud, and an optional account that backs up your favorites.

Bible Verse is a reading aid. It is not a church, not a counsellor, and not a substitute for either.

The free version

You can use Bible Verse without paying and without an account. The daily feed, the free categories, the widgets, the Lock Screen verse, reminders, the streak and the Default read aloud voice are free and stay free. The free version includes a limited number of feed items, Prayer chat messages and quiz rounds each day. Some categories, themes, voices and higher limits need Premium. We may change the free limits to keep the service running.

Prayer chat, and its limits

Prayer chat uses an AI model to write replies.

  • Replies are generated and can be wrong. Read them as a starting point, not as authority. Check anything that matters against the Bible itself and against people you trust.
  • Prayer chat is not pastoral care, counselling or therapy, and it does not give medical, legal, financial or mental health advice. Do not rely on it for any of those.
  • It does not speak for God, does not deliver personal messages, and does not predict the future. If it ever appears to, that is a malfunction, and we would like to know about it.
  • If you may be in danger, do not use the chat. If a message suggests someone is at risk, the app shows crisis support resources instead of a reply. The app cannot call anyone for you. Contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
  • We limit how many messages can be sent each day to keep the service running.

Read aloud voices

The Default voice is spoken by your iPhone. The Premium voices are generated by an AI speech model. Either may mispronounce a name or a word. Only the text you choose to hear is sent for speech.
